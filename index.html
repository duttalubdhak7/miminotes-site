MimiNotes

Handwrite • Organize • Sync

Your thoughts, beautifully organized.

MimiNotes is a personal handwriting notes app for Android. Write your notes by hand, organize them the way you like, and keep them in sync.

Sign in with Google and your notes are saved to and loaded from your own Google Drive. The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, and the app contains no ads, analytics, or third-party trackers.
