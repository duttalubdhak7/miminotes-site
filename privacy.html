Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the MimiNotes Android app ("MimiNotes", "the app") handles information. MimiNotes is a personal handwriting notes app. The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data, and the app contains no ads, analytics, or third-party trackers.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account sign-in: You sign in with your Google account. The app uses this sign-in only to authorize access to your own Google Drive.
  • Files in your Google Drive: The app reads and writes files in your own Google Drive, only to save and load your own notes.

How information is used

Information the app accesses is used only to save and load your own notes so you can view and edit them in the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Data storage and sharing

Your notes stay between your device and your own Google Drive. The developer does not receive, collect, store, sell, or share your notes or your personal data.

Google API Services

MimiNotes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not keep copies of your notes. You can delete your notes at any time in your Google Drive. You can revoke MimiNotes' access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children's privacy

MimiNotes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
